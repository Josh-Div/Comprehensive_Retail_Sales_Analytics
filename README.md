# Comprehensive_Retail_Sales_Analytics
End-to-end Retail Sales Analytics project using Python, SQL Server, and Power BI with 67 SQL queries and 7 interactive dashboards


## Project Overview

This project presents a comprehensive end-to-end retail sales analytics solution developed using Python, SQL Server, and Power BI.

The objective was to transform raw retail transaction data into meaningful business insights that support strategic decision-making. The project covers the complete analytics workflow, including data cleaning, SQL-based business analysis, KPI development, and interactive dashboard creation.

The final solution consists of **67 SQL business queries** and **7 interactive Power BI dashboards**, providing insights into sales performance, customer behavior, product performance, regional trends, profitability, and shipping efficiency.

---

## Business Problem

Retail businesses generate large volumes of transactional data every day. Without proper analysis, valuable opportunities to improve profitability, customer retention, operational efficiency, and strategic planning can be overlooked.

The objective of this project was to design a comprehensive Business Intelligence solution capable of transforming raw sales data into meaningful insights for executive decision-making.

The solution integrates Python for ETL and data cleaning, SQL Server for business analysis, and Power BI for interactive dashboard reporting.

## Power BI Dashboards

### Executive Overview

Provides a high-level overview of business performance through executive KPIs and sales trends.

### Sales Performance Analysis

Analyses monthly sales, yearly sales, profit trends, and Year-over-Year growth.

### Customer Analysis

Evaluates customer lifetime value, customer profitability, purchasing behaviour, and loss-making customers.

### Product Analysis

Identifies best-selling products, top-performing categories, product profitability, and product rankings.

### Regional Analysis

Analyses markets, regions, countries, states, and cities to identify geographical performance.

### Discount & Profitability Analysis

Evaluates profit margins, loss-making products, loss-making orders, and profitability by category.

### Shipping & Logistics Analysis

Analyses shipping modes, shipping costs, logistics efficiency, and shipping profitability.

## Key Business Insights

- Total Sales reached **R12.64 Million**
- Total Profit reached **R1.47 Million**
- Overall Profit Margin is **11.61%**
- Technology is the highest-performing category.
- APAC is the highest-performing market.
- Sales increased consistently between 2011 and 2014.
- Standard Class shipping generated the highest sales and profit.
- Several products and customers generated recurring losses.

---

## Business Recommendations

- Expand Technology product offerings.
- Invest more in high-performing markets.
- Improve customer retention strategies.
- Review pricing strategies for low-margin products.
- Eliminate consistently loss-making products.
- Optimise shipping costs.
- Continue monitoring KPIs using Power BI dashboards.
- Support strategic decision-making using SQL analytics.
